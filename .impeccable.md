## Design Context

### Users
Fleet managers and business owners across all Israeli industries (transport, agriculture, construction, services). They manage anywhere from a handful of vehicles to large fleets. Context: desktop-first at the office, mobile on the go. They are practical, time-pressed, and skeptical of overpromised SaaS. They need to trust the product before signing up.

### Brand Personality
Calm · Professional · Modern

The interface should feel like a tool built by people who understand logistics — not a startup trying to be flashy. Confidence without arrogance. Clarity without coldness.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Clean white content sections with strong typographic hierarchy
- Dark cinematic hero (not generic SaaS blue-gradient)
- Reference: oryzo.ai (floating pill nav, deliberate motion) + findrealestate.com (clean white sections, readable cards)
- Anti-reference: bubbly startup aesthetics, heavy glassmorphism, neon accents
- Hebrew-first (RTL), bilingual He/En toggle
- Font: Heebo (Hebrew/Latin — non-negotiable for RTL support)
- Primary: #2563eb blue. Neutrals tinted toward blue.

### Design Principles
1. **Trust through clarity** — every element earns its place; no decoration for decoration's sake
2. **Calm confidence** — generous whitespace, deliberate motion, no bounces or elastic easing
3. **Bilingual by default** — RTL and LTR must feel equally intentional, not bolted on
4. **Flagship quality** — this is the public face of the business; details matter
5. **No AI tells** — no gradient text, no side-stripe cards, no glassmorphism spray
