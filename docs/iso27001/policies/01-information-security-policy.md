## 1. Purpose

This Information Security Policy establishes the management direction and support for information security at Celox AI Ltd. in accordance with business requirements, relevant laws, and regulations — including the Israeli Privacy Protection Law (5741-1981) and ISO/IEC 27001:2022.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:
- All information assets owned or managed by Celox AI Ltd.
- All systems, infrastructure, and cloud services used to operate the Celox AI fleet management platform (celoxai.com)
- All personnel, contractors, and third-party suppliers with access to Celox AI systems
- All data processed on behalf of customers, including driver personal data, vehicle GPS data, and company fleet records

## 3. Information Security Objectives

Celox AI Ltd. is committed to:
1. **Confidentiality** — Ensuring that information is accessible only to those authorized to access it
2. **Integrity** — Safeguarding the accuracy and completeness of information and processing methods
3. **Availability** — Ensuring that authorized users have access to information and systems when required
4. **Legal Compliance** — Meeting all applicable legal, regulatory, and contractual requirements
5. **Continuous Improvement** — Regularly reviewing and improving the ISMS

## 4. Management Commitment

Celox AI Ltd. management (Bar Gershenson, CEO) is committed to:
- Establishing, implementing, maintaining, and continually improving the Information Security Management System (ISMS)
- Ensuring information security requirements are integrated into all business processes
- Providing adequate resources for information security
- Communicating the importance of effective information security
- Conducting regular management reviews of the ISMS

## 6. Key Principles

### 6.1 Risk-Based Approach
All information security decisions are based on identified risks. Risks are assessed, treated, and monitored in accordance with the Risk Management Policy (ISMS-POL-006).

### 6.2 Least Privilege
Users, systems, and processes are granted only the minimum level of access required to perform their function.

### 6.3 Defense in Depth
Multiple layers of security controls are implemented so that if one control fails, others remain in place.

### 6.4 Security by Design
Security is considered at the design stage of all new systems, features, and processes — not added as an afterthought.

### 6.5 Data Minimization
Only the personal data necessary for the specified purpose is collected and processed.

## 7. Compliance

All personnel must comply with this policy and all subordinate policies. Violations may result in disciplinary action and, where applicable, legal proceedings.
