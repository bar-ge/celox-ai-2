## 1. Purpose

To define acceptable use of Celox AI information systems, devices, and data — protecting the company, its customers, and individuals from harm.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all individuals who access Celox AI systems including employees, contractors, and authorized customer users.

## 3. General Principles

All users of Celox AI systems must:
- Use systems only for authorized business purposes
- Protect their login credentials and never share them
- Report security incidents or suspected vulnerabilities immediately
- Comply with all applicable laws and regulations

## 4. Permitted Uses

- Accessing the Celox AI application for legitimate fleet management purposes
- Using company devices and systems for work-related tasks
- Communicating via company-approved channels for business purposes
- Accessing customer data only within the scope of their authorized role

## 5. Prohibited Uses

The following are strictly prohibited:

### 5.1 Unauthorized Access
- Attempting to access data, systems, or accounts beyond authorized scope
- Bypassing authentication mechanisms or security controls
- Sharing login credentials with any other person
- Using another user's account

### 5.2 Data Misuse
- Copying or exporting customer data for unauthorized purposes
- Sharing RESTRICTED or CONFIDENTIAL data without authorization
- Using customer data for any purpose beyond the contracted service
- Storing personal data in unauthorized locations (local disks, personal email, etc.)

### 5.3 System Misuse
- Installing unauthorized software on company systems
- Using company systems or infrastructure for personal gain
- Mining cryptocurrency using company or customer resources
- Launching attacks, port scans, or penetration tests against systems without written authorization

### 5.4 Communication
- Sending confidential data via unencrypted email or messaging
- Impersonating Celox AI or its employees
- Making unauthorized public statements about security incidents

## 6. Personal Device Use (BYOD)

Celox AI currently operates without a formal BYOD program. The primary development device (Bar Gershenson's laptop) is treated as a company asset and must comply with all security requirements in the Access Control Policy.

Requirements for the primary workstation:
- Full-disk encryption enabled
- Screen lock after 5 minutes of inactivity
- Up-to-date OS and security patches
- Reputable antivirus/EDR software installed
- Backups of development work maintained

## 7. Internet and Social Media

- Do not share confidential company or customer information on social media
- Do not discuss unreleased features, security vulnerabilities, or customer details publicly
- Be aware that public posts can damage company reputation and violate customer NDAs

## 8. Software and Licensing

- Use only legally licensed software
- All open-source libraries used in the product must have compatible licenses (MIT, Apache, BSD preferred)
- Review npm/package dependencies for security vulnerabilities using automated scanning

## 9. Violations

Violations of this policy may result in:
- Immediate suspension of access
- Termination of employment or contract
- Legal action where applicable
- Notification to affected parties and regulators
